## External evidence rules

- `CONTACT_SENT` means only that a request was sent.
- `REPLY_RECEIVED` requires an actual inbound response.
- `PROPOSAL_RECEIVED` requires a written scope/offer from the external party.
- `ENGAGED` requires explicit commercial authorization by the owner.
- `CERTIFIED`, `ATTESTED`, `APPRAISED` or `VERIFIED` may only be used after the relevant independent third party issues or publishes that result.
